## 🌍 Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of this project, pledge to make participation in our community a **safe, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment‑free experience** for everyone—regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Our goal is not just to build software, but to build a **healthy, respectful, and collaborative community**. 💙

---

## ✅ Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a **positive environment** include:

* 💬 Using **kind, respectful, and inclusive language**
* 🤝 Being open to **different viewpoints and experiences**
* 🧠 Giving and receiving **constructive feedback** gracefully
* 🙏 Showing empathy toward other community members
* 🚀 Focusing on what is best for the **project and the community**
* 🌱 Helping newcomers learn and grow

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* ❌ Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind
* ❌ Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments
* ❌ Trolling, flaming, or deliberately provoking others
* ❌ Publishing others’ private information without permission (doxxing)
* ❌ Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
* ❌ Sustained disruption of discussions or project activities

Such behavior will **not be tolerated**. 🚨

---

## 🛡️ Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

* 🔍 Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
* ⚖️ Taking appropriate and fair corrective action when rules are violated
* 🧹 Removing or editing content that violates this Code of Conduct
* 🔒 Protecting the privacy and safety of reporters

Maintainers may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviors deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

---

## 📣 Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. 📝 Report the issue clearly and calmly
2. 📸 Include relevant links, screenshots, or context if possible
3. 🔐 Your report will be handled **confidentially and respectfully**

You can report issues by:

* Opening a **private issue** (if supported)
* Contacting the project maintainers directly

> 💡 **Do not retaliate publicly.** Reporting helps keep the community safe for everyone.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

Possible consequences include:

* 🟡 Warning
* 🟠 Temporary suspension
* 🔴 Permanent ban from the project

Enforcement decisions will be based on:

* Severity of the incident
* Context and intent
* Prior behavior
